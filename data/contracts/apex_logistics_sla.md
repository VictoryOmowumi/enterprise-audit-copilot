# Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Operating Contract
**Vendor:** Apex Logistics Global  
**Contract Code:** VEND-APEX-001  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  

## Section 1: Standard Delivery Timelines
Apex Logistics agrees to standard transit times not exceeding 48 hours for regional distribution and 72 hours for national transit. The delivery clock commences immediately upon dispatch sign-off at the manufacturing plant.

## Section 2: Delayed Delivery Penalties
If a shipment is delayed beyond the agreed timeline without prior written force-majeure notification:
- Delays between 12 and 24 hours incur a penalty of 5% of the total declared shipment value.
- Delays exceeding 24 hours incur a penalty of 15% of the declared shipment value.
- If a delay causes the cancellation of a downstream retail order, Apex Logistics shall reimburse the full transport fee plus a fixed $500 administrative disruption charge.

## Section 3: Damage and Product Integrity
Apex Logistics assumes full liability for damaged goods in transit. Any consignment received with seal breaches, broken cartons, or pallet crush exceeding 2% of unit count must be recorded on the Proof of Delivery (POD) with photo evidence within 4 hours of arrival. Reimbursement will be processed at cost value within 14 calendar days.

## Section 4: Cold Chain & Temperature Violations
For refrigerated product lines, temperatures must remain strictly between 2°C and 8°C throughout transit. Any excursion exceeding 10°C for longer than 30 continuous minutes renders the entire batch non-viable. In such cases, Apex Logistics will be liable for 100% of the consignment's declared value.
